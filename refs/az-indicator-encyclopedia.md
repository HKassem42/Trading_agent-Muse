1. Training Features for a Trading Bot (From Previous Research)
Market & Price Action Data (OHLCV)
 Candlestick Data: Open, High, Low, and Close prices over various timeframes (e.g., 1m, 5m, 1h, 1d).
 Volume: Total shares, contracts, or coins traded during the period.
 Order Book (Level 2/3) Data: Bid/ask spread, bid size, ask size, limit order distributions, and overall market depth.
 Tick Data: Individual executed trades and order flow dynamics.
Time-Based & Calendar Features
 Cyclical Features: Time of day, day of the week, and month (useful for identifying seasonal trends or specific market hour behaviors).
 Proximity Metrics: Minutes until the market opens or closes.
 Macroeconomic Events: Binary flags or countdowns for scheduled events (e.g., FOMC meetings, CPI releases, earnings calls).
Alternative & Off-Chart Data
 Sentiment Analysis: Natural Language Processing (NLP) scores derived from financial news, Twitter, Reddit, or earnings transcripts.
 On-chain Data (Crypto-specific): Hash rates, exchange inflows/outflows, active wallet addresses, and transaction fees.
 Macro Indicators: Inflation rates, interest rates, bond yields, and commodity prices (like Gold or Oil).
Statistical & Derived Features
 Moments of Distribution: Rolling means, standard deviations, skewness (asymmetry of returns), and kurtosis (tail risk).
 Z-scores: Normalizing prices to measure standard deviations from the historical mean.
 Autocorrelation: Measuring how current prices correlate with past prices.
