## The verdict, plainly

**No demonstrated signal, and the formal gate "pass" is vacuous — it
must not un-park anything.**

Two independent things went wrong-shaped:

1. The classifier's output is a whisper: on the 792-row holdout its
   probabilities span only [0.468, 0.547] (std 0.014). Accuracy at the
   0.5 cut is 0.5189 vs a 0.5051 majority baseline — +1.4 pp at
   n=792 (SE ≈ ±1.8 pp), i.e. under 1σ. The mid-sample second angle
   points the other way (below). Two angles, no consistent edge.
2. The threshold policy wrapped around it (long ≥ 0.55, short ≤ 0.45)
   therefore **never fired a single trade** in any gate window:
   100% hold everywhere, P&L $0.00, score 0.0 — which "beats" the
   champion's −2,063 on all three windows by the letter of the gate
   rule. That is the same degenerate shape as the old 15m no-trade
   convergence, arriving through the gate this time: score(flat) = 0
   beats any losing champion. The registered run's reason and a
   follow-up correction lab note state this; the duel scorer needs an
   activity floor before any future gate application (see "Gate-design
   finding" below).

## Methods

- **Data**: Coinbase BTC-USD 1h, 730d, fetched once and cached
  (`data/ha_seq/ohlcv.pkl`): 17,510 raw candles, 2024-10-22 11:00 UTC
  → 2026-10-22 10:00 UTC → 17,042 supervised rows (production
  `make_supervised`). Production split: train = first 16,250 rows;
  holdout = last **792 rows** (from 2026-09-19 11:00 UTC), gate
  windows = 3 × 264. Holdout up-rate 0.5051.
- **Inputs (no hand-crafted TA)**: per-candle channels from
  Heikin-Ashi + volume, all relative/stationary:
  `ha_ret` (log HA-close return), `body_ratio`, `upper_wick`,
  `lower_wick` (shares of the HA range), `range_rel` (HA range / HA
  close), `vol_log_rel` (log volume vs trailing-24h mean). Sequences =
  the last 90 candles ending at the decision row. Standardization
  statistics fit on fit-region candles ONLY (rows up to the last fit
  candle, raw position 14,281) and applied unchanged elsewhere.
  HA is computed on the raw frame, so sup rows carry true HA history.
- **Model**: Conv1d(6→64, k7) → pool → Conv1d(64→64, k5) → pool →
  LSTM(64→128) → dropout 0.3 → Linear(128→48) → dropout → Linear(48→1),
  **128,865 params**. BCE loss, Adam lr 1e-3, batch 256, seed 42.
  Fit on 13,813 sequences; chronological validation = last 15% of the
  train region (2,437 sequences) drove early stopping (patience 6 on
  val loss): stopped at epoch 9, best epoch 3 (val loss 0.6917, val
  acc 0.5248; peak val acc 0.5302 at epoch 4). Val loss rose from
  epoch 4 on while train loss kept falling — mild overfit, weak signal.
- **Policy wrapper**: symmetric thresholds — long when p ≥ 0.55,
  short when p ≤ 0.45, flatten between (sell if long, cover if short,
  hold if flat), expressed through the broker's position-aware action
  semantics (25% tranches, 75% exposure cap, ATR stops 2×ATR% clipped
  [2.5%, 8%], trailing arm +3% / 1.5×ATR% clipped [2%, 6%], 0.15%
  fees, next-candle-open fills). The wrapper is a genuine SB3 policy
  (module-level class; verified to reload via `PPO.load` in a fresh
  process), so grading ran through `training.nightly.evaluate_windows`
  **verbatim** — the classifier's probabilities ride in through the
  scorer's `p_up` argument, into the same obs[21] slot the stacker
  vote occupies for the champion. Identical env, broker, fills, fees,
  stops, scoring; the aggregate was recomputed from the stored window
  records and matched exactly.
- **Champion**: current PPO champion (run 17's brain) re-graded on the
  SAME windows with the current stacker champion's votes (promoted
  2026-10-22, acc 0.4659) — the only honest same-window comparison.

## Tests first (all in tests/test_ha_seq.py; suite 31/31 after)

Written before the components; all failed at collection until the
modules existed. (a) HA transform vs hand-computed values on a 4-candle
frame, exact; per-candle channels vs hand-computed ratios.
(b) Sequence causality across **every** row (seq_len 10 and 90):
full-frame sequence == truncated-frame sequence for all 160 rows.
(c) Normalization isolation: stats are the train region's (checked
against a manual slice), mutating holdout rows ×3/×10 changes no train
sequence — and a deliberately leaky global-stats variant DOES change
them (teeth check). (d) Wrapper accounting through the real
TradingEnv+PaperBroker: a decision candle closing at 111 fills at the
next candle's open (100), fee exactly 0.15% of the $25,000 tranche,
and a wick to 96.5 through the 2.5% stop fills a full exit at the stop
level 97.5 with the exit fee charged. Rule boundary cases (0.55/0.45
edges, position-dependent flattening) unit-tested directly.

One build defect was caught and fixed mid-run: grade_main initially
ran inference over the raw-length frame into a sup-length array
(crash, no results written). Diagnosed as a raw/sup alignment bug,
fixed by mapping probabilities through raw row positions, relaunched
— the phase-marked script made the retry cost nothing.

## Gate-design finding (for a joint decision)

The duel scorer's gate has no activity floor: a silent challenger
scores exactly 0, which beats any losing champion. The training env
has an activity rule (min round trips, −0.2/trip penalty in the
reward), but the gate itself reads only scores. Recommendation: any
promotion decision should additionally require the challenger to have
placed trades (e.g. round_trips_per_day > 0 in a majority of windows,
or total fills > 0) — otherwise "do nothing" can take the crown from a
bleeding champion and, worse, could satisfy the un-park rule on paper.
`scripts/build_ha_seq.py`'s duel phase now carries this guard for the
lab family. Note the Gate Tracker feed computes its pass chip from
the recorded duel numbers (delta > 0, won ≥ 2) and therefore shows
pass=1 for run 20; the run's recorded reason and the dashboard lab
notes carry the correction. Changing the feed's formula is a
gate-rule change, left for Kass's call.

## What phase 2 would be (only if the signal were real)

It isn't, on this evidence — so this section is for the record, not a
recommendation. Had the classifier shown a demonstrated edge (holdout
AND mid-sample above baseline, probabilities with real spread), phase
2 would wire its probability into the PPO observation slot currently
occupied by the dead stacker vote (diagnosis Exp 2: that channel's
cross-fitted std is 0.014 — the same order as this classifier's
output spread, which is itself a warning), retrain the PPO against
it, and duel the result. Given v1's numbers, the honest next questions
are the ones already on the table: the A–Z encyclopedia screening
program (a different feature universe for the stacker/PPO line), a
timeframe change, or accepting the null on 1h BTC and letting the
nightly/Sunday hunters keep their watch.
