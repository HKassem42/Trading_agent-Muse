## Pre-registered design (frozen before any grading — one shot, no tuning sweeps)

### The thesis

Thirteen clean nulls share one anatomy: every directional PPO
bled in falling windows, because direction prediction on 1h BTC
is a coin flip (the two-step hierarchy study: direction
balanced accuracy ~0.50, while TRADEABILITY was predictable at
~0.61). So Pick #3's brain **stops choosing direction
entirely**. Direction is outsourced to the stacker vote; the
brain learns the one axis that showed signal -- **how much
exposure to carry, and when to stand aside**.

### Brain — "Muse's Pick #3 PPO" (exposure manager)

- **Direction source: run 73's vote ONLY** --
  direction = sign(vote - 0.5) at the decision candle (a vote
  of exactly 0.5 is direction 0: HALF/FULL then target flat).
  Vote strength reaches the policy through the state, never
  through a directional action.
- **Actions, Discrete(4)** -- exposure targets, rebalanced at
  the execution price:
  - 0 **STAND ASIDE**: target exposure 0 -- close any open
    position in full.
  - 1 **HALF**: target 37.5% of equity in the vote's direction.
  - 2 **FULL**: target 75% of equity (the program cap) in the
    vote's direction.
  - 3 **HOLD**: keep the current position untouched this step,
    even across a vote flip; exits only via the protective
    stop or the time-stop.
  Rebalances and flips execute at next-candle open in grading
  (the duel standard) and at the decision close in training
  (the production training timing, as in every prior study).
  A flip closes through flat, then opens the new side in the
  same step. Sizing goes through the production accounting:
  0.15% fee per side, the $10 minimum ticket (a rebalance whose
  |delta| is under $10 is skipped; full closes always execute),
  buy notionals grossed up by 1/(1-fee) so the RESULTING
  position value lands on the target exactly, as the broker's
  own buy accounting deducts the fee from quantity
  (implemented by setting the broker's tranche caps for that
  one fill so its own spend math lands there; sells/covers
  use the broker's pct parameter -- exact by construction --
  and short opens land on exact notional the same cap way).
- **Challenger exit config (simulation/duel/training ONLY --
  production broker.py is untouched)**, via the size-study /
  exit-study broker-parameter precedent (a PaperBroker subclass
  shadowing the exit constants per instance):
  - initial stop **1.5 x ATR%**, floored at **2%**, capped **6%**
    (production: 2.0x, [2.5%, 8%]);
  - trailing arms at **+2%** (production +3%), trail
    **1.25 x ATR%**, floored **1.5%**, capped **5%**
    (production: 1.5x, [2%, 6%]);
  - **72-hour max-hold time-stop**: a position whose age reaches
    72 candles is flattened at the step's execution price
    before the step's action applies (reason "time_stop").
    Age is measured in candles from the entry fill candle.
    (In the duel's 24-candle grading episodes the time-stop
    cannot bind; it binds in the 264-candle training episodes.
    Recorded here so the asymmetry is described, not smuggled.)
- **State (102 wide, identical to Pick #2 for
  comparability)**: the 98 scored features (s/50 - 1) + run
  73's vote centered (2p - 1) + the env's 3 portfolio readings.
  Training consumes run 73's CROSS-FITTED votes (pick recipe,
  expanding TimeSeriesSplit) for direction and state; the
  holdout uses the fitted champion's votes.
- **Reward: the standard dense per-step mark-to-market %
  (log-return, clipped [-1, 1]) + terminal +1/-1 on the
  episode's P&L sign. NO drawdown shaping** (tested at two
  horizons in Picks #1/#2, closed). One deliberate deviation
  from TradingEnv's reward, frozen here: **no activity
  penalty** -- standing aside is this brain's first-class
  decision, and a round-trip quota would pay it to churn
  against its own thesis. Round trips are still counted and
  reported.
- **Training**: SB3 PPO fresh, 60,000 steps, seed 42,
  ent_coef 0.05, episodes of 264 candles (the DuelLength
  precedent), telemetry callback ON. The env is swapped into
  ppo_train's env slot for this study's train call only
  (the Pick #2 precedent); the production env, the nightly
  retrain and both seats are never touched.

### The duel

- Challenger pair: Pick #3 brain + run 73 eyes (fitted votes).
- Champion pair: run 44 PPO artifact on its native 25-wide
  production state with **run 73's fitted holdout votes**
  plugged as its vote input, production broker parameters,
  graded by nightly.evaluate_windows exactly as in Pick #2.
- 3 chronological windows x 10 episodes, next-candle-open
  fills, score = P&L - 0.5 x maxDD per window, aggregate =
  mean of window scores. Formula: aggregate beats champion
  AND >= 2/3 windows. Floor: gate_decision()'s two-part floor
  on the challenger record (non-hold >= 10% -- "hold" here is
  the HOLD-action share, the env's only literal no-op; P&L
  > $0; P&L > buy-and-hold).

## Results (2026-10-27, appended after the run — design above was frozen first)

**Verdict: LOSS — the fourteenth clean null. Champion kept; nothing installed; the live seat was never touched.**

| 3 | −160.82 | −$9.06 | −1,100.74 | −$831.12 | −$1,164.91 |
| **Aggregate** | **−35.59** | **+$212.21** | **−415.89** | **−$453.23** | **−$5,152.50** |

- Windows won: **1/3** (the rising window only). Formula
  (agg beats champion AND ≥2/3 windows): **FAIL** — so
  `gate_decision`'s floor was never reached (floor_fail null);
  for the record the challenger's P&L was negative, so the
  floor's P&L leg would have failed too. Its non-hold share
  (last window) was 25.4%, above the floor's 10% leg.
- Last-window action mix: HOLD 74.6%, STAND ASIDE 19.6%,
  HALF 5.0%, FULL 0.8%. Round trips/day 0.267 vs the
  champion's 0.067. Max drawdown (worst window): $675.80 vs
  the champion's $303.52.
- Telemetry captured (118 rollouts; entropy 1.377 → 0.735;

- Registered as **run 75** (family `muse_pick3`, promoted=0).
  Guard hashes after the study are bit-identical to before it:
  the study touched nothing outside its own lab directory.

**Reading.** The exposure manager learned the shape of the
thesis — it mostly holds or stands aside, and it won the rising
window by more than the champion did (+$550 vs +$291). But
exposure sizing cannot fix a coin-flip direction: in the two
falling windows its positions bled −$172 and −$831, worse than
the champion's −$70 and −$9, because when it did ride the vote
it rode it bigger. Tradeability timing did not separate the
windows the way the hierarchy study's fragment suggested it
might. Direction remains the binding constraint, and this
brain — by design — had no direction of its own.
